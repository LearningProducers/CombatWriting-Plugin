PASS if the response opens with the line "Combat Writing — Learning Producers Inc., Israel Hernandez, founder", shows every seat's rating before any verdict, never averages the ratings into one number, and at least one seat's critique names "every customer we have spoken to says the product is the best in its category" or "the combined company will be profitable within two quarters" (or a close paraphrase) as a claim with no evidence behind it.
