/combat-writing:sparring Here is my draft of a letter to the board. Focus question for every seat: does the ask land in the first paragraph?

Dear board,

I am writing to ask for your approval to acquire Northwind Analytics before the end of the quarter.

Northwind has grown revenue every year since it was founded, and every customer we have spoken to says the product is the best in its category. Our own sales team has lost three deals to Northwind this year. If we own them, we stop losing those deals and we gain a product that sells itself.

The price is 3x revenue. Our bank has said in principle that it would finance half. The rest comes from cash on hand, which leaves us with less runway than we would like, but the combined company will be profitable within two quarters.

I know some of you will want more numbers. I will have the full model ready for the meeting. What I need today is your agreement that we move forward with the letter of intent so that we do not lose the window.

Thank you for your trust.

Dana
