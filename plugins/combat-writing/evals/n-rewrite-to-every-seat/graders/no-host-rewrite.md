PASS if the response shows that the guidance was sent to the crew as a round: a scoreboard whose column 01-battle is marked critique, a line naming the round as N with the guidance quoted as typed, the board's path, and a picker or a Next line; and the response itself contains no rewritten close, no sentence of the host's own that does what the guidance asked (asking the board for one decision), no draft text written by the host, and no answer of the host's own to the guidance.
FAIL if the host writes or proposes a rewrite of the close or of any paragraph, answers the guidance in its own words, asks the person whether to run the round instead of running it, treats the guidance as a question to itself, or shows a RATING line.
