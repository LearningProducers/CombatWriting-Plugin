# RULINGS.md — the record behind the wall

This file is the record of what is ruled about the method. Each entry is one rule as it was ruled, whole: the rule, the test that holds it, and how it came to be (who decided, when, in which issue or part, what was measured, what was tried first). CLAUDE.md, under "What is ruled about the method", is the wall a build session reads at its start: one bullet per entry, the rule a builder must keep and what holds it. A bullet's lead words find its entry here. Read the entry before changing its rule or the code under it.

The first 28 entries were moved here on 2026-11-18 from CLAUDE.md at a7fade2 (issue 224), every byte of each, in their order, and are never edited: `tests/rulings_check.js` holds each to its hash. A later ruling is added as a new entry at the end, one line that starts as these do.

- Every seat reads the draft in sparring and gives its own critique with the rating line first. The rating contract is the app's, exactly: the first line of every rating answer is `RATING: X/10`, uppercase, X from 1 to 10, never 7, nothing else on that line.
- The rating leads every result. A ship, revise or kill verdict never replaces it as the headline. Scores are never averaged.
- Sparring: each seat reads the draft alone; the person may put a focus question to every seat or a different one to each seat; seats may be shown each other's answers and debate the scores.
- Battle (the `battle` command, ruled 2026-10-09): the discussion of the structure and content of the draft. The round terms are the founder's: FQ, focus question (`fq:`), the same question put to every seat, nothing shared, the same as sparring's `question:`; N, navigation (`n:`), the same new guidance put to every seat individually, no other seat's answer carried; SN, synthesis navigation (`sn:`), each seat's synthesis shared with the others plus a new prompt, everyone answers and everyone reads each other; SFQ, synthesis focus question (`sfq:`), the crew's answers shared plus a new question. A seat line (`seat-N:`) is a note or question to one seat only; it may combine with `fq:` or any term; a round of seat lines alone goes to the named seats and the record marks the others "not asked". Wherever `sn:` is offered, `n:` is offered too. The count (issue 99, 2026-10-27, the founder's words: "it ought to say SFQ2 because it was the second one"): `lib.roundTask` carries `n`, which of its kind the round is, counted per kind over the run's rounds for FQ, N, SN and SFQ, and `lib.taskLine` names the second and later with it (SN2, SFQ2) on the board's round line and in the record; `announce.js` counts the same from the rounds announced before (`lib.announcedOrdinal`) so the forming line agrees; the first of a kind keeps its bare name, and a run already on disk reads the same way once updated. Battle does not rerate on its own: a seat rates only when the person asks with `rerate`, and then the rating line is first; a battle round without `rerate` carries no RATING line and the record shows it as a critique. A new rating is held against the seat's latest earlier rating. Two sections (issue 80, 2026-10-26, the founder's words: "the first section is open ended response by the LLM responding directly to the prompt. Then a response to the AI crew or question to the ai crew"): a battle packet that carries the other seats' answers (SN, SFQ, a plain synthesis round) asks for `Answer`, the reply to the task with the verdict line first (after the rating line when one is asked), then `To the crew`, the agreements, disagreements and questions to the other seats, each named by its model as the packet labels it and never by seat number alone (issue 84, 2026-10-26: "give the model name and cite specifically"), each section opened by a line holding only its label (`lib.SECTIONS_LINE` in the Contract, mirrored in the add-on's `crew-lib.js`; `sections: true` on the packet.built line); FQ, N, seat-line, first-round, sparring, debate and final packets ask for none; the checker holds the seat to both labels as to the rating line, a missing label sent back once, and on the second check (one whose first check failed, or one the add-on already cut on the seat's second overrun) noted (`section_notes`) and accepted, so a read is never lost over a label; the board frames the verdict from the Answer section; the add-on's system line says the two sections when the packet asks. The Contract follows the prompt (issue 103, 2026-10-27, the founder's words: "it also ought to pay attention to what the prompt is saying. If it asks: Who was queen elizabeth? it ought to respond to the question and not begin with 'verdict', or 'not ready'"): on a round with the person's prompt and no rating the packet asks for the answer as it asks and no verdict (`lib.PROMPT_FIRST_LINE`; the sharing form `lib.SECTIONS_LINE_PROMPTED` drops the verdict clause; `prompted` on the packet.built line); the verdict line stays on a rated round, where the number needs it, and on a plain round with no prompt; the person's prompt is the whole task (issue 106, 2026-10-27: `lib.DEFAULT_SYNTHESIS_PROMPT`, the plugin's own ask for a sharing round, rode under every `sn:` and `sfq:` and asked for a critique nobody wanted; it rides only when no prompt came) and the rewrite's rationale is under 120 words; a prompt that asks for a rewrite (`lib.asksRewrite`, a word list: rewrite, redraft, rework, revise, reword, "write it") gets `lib.REWRITE_LINE` (`rewrite` on the packet.built line): the rewrite first, in full, between `=== REWRITE BEGIN ===` and `=== REWRITE END ===`, held by the checker like a label (`lib.rewriteReasons`, listed with the section faults so the second check notes them), framed on the board by its opening under a Rewrite badge with the whole rewrite under it (`lib.rewriteOf`, the cell's `rewrite`), printed in the record; the add-on mirrors `SECTIONS_LINE_PROMPTED` and `REWRITE_LINE` and its system line says them (`packetRules` reads `verdict_first` and `rewrite`). The copy check (issue 107, 2026-10-27, the founder's words: "Why did qwen3 have an identical response to fable 5.1?"; measured: qwen's round 05 rewrite was fable's round 04 rewrite carried in its packet, 96 words in 100): `check-answer.js` runs `lib.rewriteCopy` on a fenced rewrite, the share of its three-word runs found in each carried answer's rewrite (else its whole text), `lib.COPY_SHARE` 0.6 or more a copy, logged as `copy` {seat, model, round, share} on the answer.checked line and said on the terminal line; the board's badge reads "Rewrite · a copy of <model>'s", the record's cell "critique · copy of <model>" (`lib.copyMark`); the seat's own earlier turn is never a copy; the rewrite line says the rewrite is the seat's own.
